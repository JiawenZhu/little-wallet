## 🎭 The Human Tone: Why it Works
Instead of enterprise buzzwords (*"streamlined group financial ecosystem"*), this script speaks like a real person sharing a clever trick with a friend:
- **Relatable Pain**: *"Splitting dinner with friends always starts fun — until the check lands, someone pulls out napkin math, and that one friend swears they'll Venmo you tomorrow."*
- **The "For Them" Mission**: *"The people sharing tacos and iced lattes? They just want to hang out. So we built Little Wallet, for them."*
- **Concrete & Delightful**: *"No forms. No dropdowns. You just say it... Honestly, it's faster than pulling out a credit card."*
- **Playful Observation**: *"Now, the part where group chats usually go to die: actually getting paid back."*
- **The Magic Moment**: *"And here's our favorite little detail: the second Sam taps pay on PayPal, his badge flips from Waiting to Paid instantly. Live webhooks. It just knows."*
- **Smart Agent Autonomy**: *"Meet Penny. If someone forgets, Penny drafts an official PayPal Invoice so it doesn't feel awkward... She never touches your money without your confirmation."*
